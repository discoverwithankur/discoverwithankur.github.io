Welcome to **Discover With Ankur** — my academic portfolio and digital archive.

This platform documents my learning journey through analytical essays, research notes, policy observations, and reflections on history, geopolitics, governance, and the forces shaping our world.

## Areas of Interest

* **History & Statecraft:** Exploring political institutions, civilizations, historical processes, and lessons from the past that influence modern governance.

* **Geopolitics & International Relations:** Studying diplomacy, strategic affairs, global power shifts, regional dynamics, and India's role in the changing world order.

* **Public Policy & Governance:** Understanding institutions, administrative systems, economic reforms, development strategies, and governance challenges.
